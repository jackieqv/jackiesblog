## Ingredients

- Beef for soup (ca. 500g)
- Soup vegetables
- Udon noodles (or Ramen noodles)
- neutral oil (e.g. sunflower oil)
- salt
- pepper


## Method

1. Preheat oven to 180 degrees top/bottom heat. Mash the bananas with a fork in a bowl.
2. Add oil, brown sugar and eggs to the bananas and whisk.
3. Mix the flour, baking powder, salt, cinnamon, etc. and mix with the oil-egg mixture.
4. Place a sheet of baking paper in the loaf pan and put in the dough. Bake in the preheated oven for 50 minutes.
5. Let the banana loaf cool completely after baking. Enjoy!
